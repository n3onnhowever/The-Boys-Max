# T040 — Product naming + brand system
Recommended: GPT-5.6 Sol / medium

Goal
Produce a decision-ready naming and visual-system proposal compatible with the The Boys team logo, the MAX context and the personal-event-guide product.

Inputs
- docs/product/PRODUCT_FRAME.md
- docs/design/NAMING_WORKSPACE.md
- docs/design/BRAND_BRIEF.md
- assets/brand/team-logo.* (once placed)
- official case
- runtime screenshots from T020 when available

Do
1. Inventory/sample the original team logo asset; preserve team-brand provenance.
2. Generate and screen a compact naming set. Do not treat creative generation as availability clearance.
3. For finalists, check obvious confusion/availability across MAX nickname, web/app/GitHub and major Russian trademark/domain signals where accessible.
4. Build 2–3 coherent visual directions, each with product palette, neutrals, typography, event-photo treatment and MAX compatibility.
5. Check Cyrillic, font licences and contrast.
6. Recommend one direction for team review, but label it PROPOSED until approved.
7. Produce design tokens and a one-page designer handoff that can later drive both mini-app and presentation.

Do not
- register irreversible MAX bot nickname;
- alter the supplied team logo;
- sample final HEX values from a chat-compressed preview if the original asset is unavailable;
- copy closed-product visual assets.

Acceptance
The team receives a small decision set with evidence, not dozens of names/palettes.
