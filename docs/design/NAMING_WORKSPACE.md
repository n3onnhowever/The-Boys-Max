# Naming workspace

**Historical naming research.** Current accepted product is **Повод**, team **The Boys**. The former TBD state and candidate table below are preserved as history, not active naming instructions. No real MAX bot nickname change is authorized.

## Stronger candidates to research
These are creative candidates, not availability/legal conclusions.

| Candidate | Why it fits | Main risk |
|---|---|---|
| **Туда** | Directional, short, works for solo discovery and “let's go there” | Very common word; availability/distinctiveness likely difficult |
| **Куда** | Directly expresses the user problem | Extremely generic; likely crowded |
| **Внеплан** | Memorable, leisure/spontaneity tone | Can sound like lack of planning |
| **Находка** | Discovery-first, positive | Less connected to group planning/maps |
| **Лови** | Energetic recommendation/discovery verb | Broad/common; may feel too informal |
| **Сюда** | Location/navigation feel, short CTA | Generic and directional rather than event-specific |
| **Пойдём** | Natural bridge from discovery to friends | More group-first than solo-first |
| **Выход** | “Going out” and action; compact | Many existing meanings/brands |
| **Радар** | Discovery metaphor, scalable beyond events | Common product/feature name |
| **В моменте** | Leisure and spontaneity, youth tone | Longer; trend-language may age |
| **Затея** | Activity/idea for free time, friendly Russian word | Less obviously an event navigator |
| **Повод** | An event gives a reason to go out; broad | Semantically indirect |
| **Двигай** | Energy/action, youth | Too informal for some audiences |
| **Курс** | Navigation/action | Weak connection to leisure |
| **Событийно** | Direct event association | Artificial/less natural word |

## Historical naming gate
Before approval, research at minimum:
- MAX bot nickname availability;
- Russian web/app-store/GitHub confusion;
- major Russian trademark/domain conflicts as a screening step;
- pronunciation/spelling in Russian;
- fit for both solo and collaborative paths;
- presentation/logo legibility.

Do not treat the table order as a ranking. Final selection belongs to the team.
